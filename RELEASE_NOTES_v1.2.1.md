# HABridge 1.2.1

HABridge 1.2.1 makes preamp control in eMotion LV1 smoother and more reliable, and simplifies installing and updating HABridge Control.

## What's new

- Gain knobs keep a correct scale when the console, S32 or SoundGrid card is detected after LV1 starts.
- Knob rotation is smooth and regular; one step equals one real gain step.
- An unavailable port keeps its last value and receives no command.
- An S32 is still recognised when another console is chained on its port B.
- DN32-WSG: associations from HABridge Control are used on Windows; DN32-WSG is recognised on macOS.
- HABridge Control: new icon, Start menu shortcut (Windows), single window, clearer device list, fixed Advanced search and manual connection, update and uninstall work whatever the installed version.

## Updating from 1.2.0

Close eMotion LV1 and HABridge Control, open the new version, then click « Installer cette version ». Associations are kept.

## Notes

- Original Waves SoundGrid modules must already be installed.
- HABridge does not include or redistribute Waves modules.
- Close eMotion LV1 before installing or updating HABridge.

Before enabling or recalling +48 V, verify the microphone, source and cabling connected to the preamp.
